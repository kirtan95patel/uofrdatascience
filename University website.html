Two ways to study data science

Whether you are starting university or upgrading your skills, there is a path that fits.

Undergraduate

Bachelor of Science in Data Science

  • Four-year degree offered jointly by Computer Science and Mathematics & Statistics.
  • Combines analytics, machine learning, programming, and AI.
  • The only undergraduate data science degree in Saskatchewan.
  • Available through the University of Regina and its federated colleges (Campion, First Nations University of Canada, Luther).
Graduate

MSc in Computer Science (Data Science)

  • Course-based degree: 30 credits, no thesis required.
  • Typically two years full-time.
  • Focus on data-focused programming, the data science lifecycle, statistics, and machine learning.
  • Designed for new graduates and for professionals moving into data work.

What you'll learn

Data science sits where mathematics, statistics, and computer science meet. Our curriculum follows the full data science lifecycle, from raw data to a clear recommendation.

Programming for data Statistics & probability Machine learning Data mining Data preparation & cleaning Visualization & presentation Artificial intelligence Communicating results

Explore & prepare

Collect, clean, and structure messy real-world data so it is ready for analysis.

Model & predict

Apply statistical methods and machine learning to find patterns and make predictions.

Visualize & communicate

Present findings so that non-technical decision makers can understand and act on them.

Why study data science at Regina?

Built with industry

The programs were developed through a partnership with Saskatchewan Government Insurance (SGI), which funded a post-doctoral fellowship to design the course content.

An interdisciplinary team

Computer Science and Mathematics & Statistics teach the bachelor's degree together, so you get both computing and statistical depth.

A long record of quality

The Department of Computer Science has been continuously accredited by the Computer Science Accreditation Council since 1982.

Research labs

Computer Science facilities include specialized labs, such as a Data Mining Lab, where students can work on hands-on projects.

How we fit in the Canadian landscape

We looked at three leading Canadian programs while designing ours. Each takes a different approach, and we borrowed ideas from all of them.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a computer science background?

Requirements differ by program. Check the University of Regina admissions pages for the current prerequisites for the BSc and for the MSc (Data Science).

Can I transfer into the Data Science program?

Yes. Students can transfer from another U of R program or from other colleges and universities, provided they meet Faculty of Science admission requirements.

Is the MSc a thesis degree?

No. The MSc in Computer Science (Data Science) is course-based, with 30 credits of coursework.

What careers can I pursue?

Graduates work as data analysts, data scientists, machine learning specialists, and researchers across industries such as insurance, energy, finance, and government.

Where do I apply?

Visit the undergraduate or graduate program page on the University of Regina website.
